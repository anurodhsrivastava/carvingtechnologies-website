About the studio

Built for complexity.
Driven by craft.

Carving Technologies is a software engineering studio specializing in technically demanding products. We work across the full stack — from training custom ML models and building computer-vision pipelines to shipping polished mobile apps and real-time multiplayer systems.

Our portfolio spans AI coaching platforms with custom-trained pose-estimation models, peer-to-peer encrypted messaging, cross-platform TV apps, GPS activity tracking, RAG-powered assistants, and developer tooling. Every project reflects a commitment to clean architecture, rigorous testing, and thoughtful UX.

Whether it's an MVP for a startup or a complex system for an established business, we bring the same rigor: understand the problem deeply, choose the right tools, and ship software that works.
